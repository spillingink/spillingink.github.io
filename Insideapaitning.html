Inside a painting
By:Dhristi Desai

In a big and bustling city at least one person is always unhappy. In this case it was Maria. Her husband had died, a tragic event. But they had known it was coming. She was not crying because all her tears had been dried. Yet still unhappy. So, Maria dressed in black, and went to meet the lawyer. Her husband trusted this lawyer, always confidential. As she walked through the dark hallway with a few paintings everywhere. Then she reached the lawyers door. She saw a chair and sat there. At the same time, the lawyer had a car crash, but no one knew where he was going. He was, after all, confidential. Maria was a patient woman, therefore never noticed all the time that had gone by, sometimes people walked or waved. Maria always waved back. But no informed her the lawyer was dead. So there she sat, a patient woman, waiting for a dead lawyer.

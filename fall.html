Fall
By:Dhristi Desai

Swirling

Twirling

All around

Little pieces, flying round.

And in the center of it all

You stand, amazed by the fall.

They go in shapes, and colors all

If only we could rake them all

Then lift them up high, high

Higher than the autumn sky.

A Walls Life
By:Dhristi Desai

The girl was in a hurry,

A hurry again.

Just like the other days.

When she left, the room was empty

The girl was the inhabitant of the room.

For now.

We watched her grow like spectators

She was getting older

But not old enough

Soon she would move out and give it to someone else

Just like the other times

We were here before her

and we'll be here after her

But we were lucky too

Rumor has it one wall was knocked down

To the pieces

Poor wall

Sometimes the girl needs help

We try to help her

But our whispers never reach her

She is not connected to her soul

Sometimes she is sad

Sometimes she is happy

Humans are hard to understand

Walls are not.

I like to think we don't gang up, and are peaceful

But walls are not like that, it's not in our nature

I like to think we are caring, and kind

But we are not.

I guess we too have a lot to learn.

Just like humans

My favorite inhabitant was named Mira

She was a wonderful, kind hearted soul.

And she heard us

But time snatched her away from us too soon

And just like that she was gone.

I have not gotten attached since

People think we control faith

At least, I like to think they do

But we don't

Faith controls us, and time has plans for us too

When life gets too old for me, I'll be ready

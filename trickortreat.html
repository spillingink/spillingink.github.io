Trick Or Treat
By:Dhristi Desai

Trick or treat,

What candy do you seek?

Is it chocolate or a lolly?

Or maybe a gummy?

Trick or treat,

What is your costume tonight,

On this dark, dark night?

Is it spooky like a ghost,

Or not, like a chicken roast?

Trick or treat,

Look at all your candy,

Full of joy and taffy,

And you look so happy!

A Soccer Game
By:Dhristi Desai

Some people go down into the hall of fame for everyone to see, some go down in your heart, where you will always see. It was cold, fingers were numb. But hope was evident. We were giving it our all. Every ounce of strength, every muscle strained, and in the mist of it all, somewhere along the line, it clicked, like lightning. Who knew one pass could make your day? Who knew? It changed mine. As a crowd surrounded her, she kicked and it went in. Goal. Wow. Who knew? But it wasn’t done. More was to come. Throughout that whole hour a mix of emotions would go through me, and it had only just begun. 1-1. Tied, for now. They struck back, quickly, hungry for revenge, for one last game to win. We fought, but it simply wasn’t enough, and the ball went in. 1-2. Hope was still there; with 30 more minutes anything could happen. The clock was ticking, the game was going, it was on. We were hungry for something we hadn’t tasted in a while, a win. We had chances, and lost them, but sometime one of these chances would become a miracle. We knew it, so did they. They fought hard, but it was our turn. Finally, our turn. We held our breath, one kick, one blink, and it would either go one way or another. Which way would it choose, it went ours. Breathing a sigh of relief, we ran back to our positions. Excitement tingling in our body. 2-2. Tied. Most goals ever scored this whole season. Almost unbelievable. By now, I believed we could do anything, anything. But now, we were done celebrating. No more mistakes. They would not get through us. Not again. Before I continue, I have to tell you something. Determination can do many things; you can achieve much with it. But at the end of the day, determination only works because you gain talent from consistency. Unfortunately, in this case, we did not have just enough talent. It seemed like they wouldn’t get through us, but one kick, giant and high, a little too high. Our goalie is very good, extremely good. She jumps, dives, she didn’t go down without a fight. She jumped. High. Sometimes we lack talent, she lacked height. It wasn’t her fault. We knew that, 3-2. At this point I just wanted to tie, a pleasure we also hadn’t had. We wanted it badly, but we failed to grasp all our chances. So many chances, and all of a sudden, the whistle blew, time was up. My wishes, gone, theirs, fulfilled. But at the end of the day, someone has to win. Perhaps today wasn’t our time. Yet. At least in the end, we were greeted with a sugary surprise, doughnuts to celebrate us. In the end, we didn’t win, but in my heart, we won, and sometimes that’s all that matters. Not what they think, but to know that we pushed ourselves like never before. We did better than ever. That, is enough. Because soon enough it will be our turn.
